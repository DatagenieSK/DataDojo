# DataDojo Code of Conduct 🥋

## Our Pledge
We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, encouraging, and supportive environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Dojo Principles
- **Mistakes are normal and celebrated**: Learning to code can feel intimidating. We meet beginner questions with empathy and clarity.
- **Kindness first**: Constructive feedback is warm and actionable.
- **Respectful collaboration**: We listen actively and treat every contributor with respect.
