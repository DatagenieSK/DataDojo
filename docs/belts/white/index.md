# White Belt: Start Here ⚪

The White Belt is where every coder begins. In this belt, you will learn how to write instructions for a computer, store information, and solve mistakes with confidence.

---

| Kata | Title | Focus | Status |

| **01** | [Your First Line of Python](01-hello-dojo.md) | Say hello and run code | **Available** |

| **03** | Numbers and Quick Math | Addition, counters, and scores | Coming soon |
| **04** | Strings and Text Tricks | Joining words and uppercase text | Coming soon |

---

## 🏅 Belt Grading: The Final Challenge

When you complete all 8 katas, you will face the **White Belt Grading**: a live sparring test combining your new techniques. Passing the grading awards you your official **Yellow Belt**!

*(Grading unlocks once all White Belt katas are complete).*
